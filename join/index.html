Join 4-H

To join Zenith, you must first be enrolled in 4-H. All children ages 5-18 are eligible to join 4-H. The cost for joining 4-H is $10 per year per club member, with a total family maximum of $30.

If you are already enrolled in 4-H, you can skip to the next step.
