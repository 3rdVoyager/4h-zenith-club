Learn About Our Club

The 4-H Zenith Club is a community of homeschooling 4-H families (and those with flexible schedules) growing together through hands-on projects, leadership, and service to others.

We meet on the third Thursday of the month from 3-5 PM or on MCPS days off in Gaithersburg or Derwood, MD.
